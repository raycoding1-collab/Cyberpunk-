## What these worlds do

**Scale comes from cues, not size.** BR2049's LA skyline is simply enormous, featureless, buttressed slabs fading into amber haze. They read as kilometre-scale only because of the tiny spinners in front of them. The archive corridor puts two specks of people at the bottom of an infinite grid. Night City puts flying cars and a tiny freeway in front of its towers. Every one of the strong frames has an object of known size.

**Depth is layered and graded by haze.** Each layer back is lighter, lower in contrast and shifted toward the air colour: teal in Night City at night, amber in LA 2049, salmon in Ghostrunner. A frame typically has 3–5 layers: a dark foreground frame (overhang, railing or car), a detailed midground, a backdrop city and far silhouettes.

**Windows are tiny and countless.** In the night key art the towers are dark masses covered in thousands of pin-point lights in one cool colour, with occasional warm floors. Big, bright window rectangles make a building look small.

**Detail density is uneven on purpose.** There are large quiet planes (BR2049 slabs, Pacifica concrete) next to dense clusters (signage stacks, rooftop machinery, balcony fringes). Uniform detail everywhere is what reads as procedural.

**Signature forms are layered, not boxes.** Downtown towers are a slab plus a cantilevered crown with a different material, X-braced façades, drum elements and lattice masts. The mall wears a geodesic lattice offset from its mass, the same move as our exoskeleton.

**Surfaces carry history.** Colour blocking (Pacifica teal, mustard stripes), rain streaks under ledges, decals, numbers, warning stencils and worn paint edges are everywhere. Clean, single-colour shells look like a demo.

**Light: one dominant motivation plus accents.** BR2049 uses a single colour with one hard shaft or caustic pattern. Night City uses a teal ambient sky and magenta/purple signs. Vehicles are rim-lit so their silhouette pops from the city behind. The sky is never a flat colour: it is a gradient lit from below by the city.

**Holograms are light in fog, not surfaces.** Joi is saturated, translucent and scan-lined, and she bleeds colour into the haze around her.

**Weather is restrained.** Rain and smoke shape the light (haze, wet reflections) but rarely cover the frame. Wet ground mirrors the light sources.

**Composition:** low wide angles with a strong foreground frame, Dutch tilt on vehicles, leading lines through repetition (torii gates, archive rows), and a hero against a lighter background.

## Applied to the Capsule Spire

1. Backdrop city in three haze layers: midfield sculpted towers with pin-point windows, horizontal neon bands and vertical screens, plus BR2049-style megastructures that rise through the cloud deck.
2. Air traffic (spinners with head- and tail-lights) on lanes around the tower, as the scale cue.
3. Sky and clouds graded deep teal, with warm glow only where the tower lights the cloud base.
4. Capsule shells with colour blocking, painted stripes, rain streaks and stencilled unit numbers.
5. Smaller, dimmer, more varied core windows.
6. Subtler rain: fewer, thinner and darker streaks.
7. Hover-car: slim ducted nacelles instead of round pods, no glowing discs, and a rim-lit hero shot in front of the city (after the 2018 key art).
